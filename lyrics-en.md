# Translated Song Lyrics (English)

This document lists the English translations for all song lyrics.
The first 13 songs follow the exact ordering of the presets inside [agost.js](file:///d:/dev/Batec/Batec/visualizer/presets/agost.js) for the live show setlist.

> [!NOTE]
> **Stage Projections Timing:** Each preset waits **30 seconds** during the intro before displaying lyric lines, giving time for the visual ambiance and band to establish. Lyrics automatically **stop displaying after 240 seconds (4 minutes)**.
> Lines are subtle, poetic sentences drawn directly from the authentic song lyrics (translated into English) to give evocative hints rather than overt story explanations.
